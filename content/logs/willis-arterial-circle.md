First-pass verification from the October 2026 accuracy review (docs/research/accuracy-review-2026-10.md), then claim-by-claim against the sources listed.

- **c1** established: Thomas Willis's Cerebri anatome (1664) gave the first complete description of the ring of arteries at the base of the brain, with illustrations mostly by Christopher Wren.
- **c2** established: Earlier anatomists had described parts of the arrangement; Willis was the first to describe it completely and assign it a function.
- **c3** established: The name "circle of Willis" was attached later, not by Willis himself.
