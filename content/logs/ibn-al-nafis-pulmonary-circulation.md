First-pass verification from the October 2026 accuracy review (docs/research/accuracy-review-2026-10.md), then claim-by-claim against the sources listed.

- **c1** established: In his commentary on the anatomy of Ibn Sina's Canon, Ibn al-Nafis rejected the idea that blood passes through pores in the septum and described it travelling from the right ventricle through the pulmonary artery and lungs, returning by the pulmonary vein.
- **c2** established: The manuscript was rediscovered in Berlin in 1924 by the Egyptian physician Muhyo al-Deen Altatawi.
- **c3** contested: Whether his description reached sixteenth-century European anatomists is debated; one study raises the possibility that his work was a source for the European rediscovery.
