First-pass verification from the October 2026 accuracy review (docs/research/accuracy-review-2026-10.md), then claim-by-claim against the sources listed.

- **c1** established: In August 1677 the medical student Johan Ham showed Leeuwenhoek small moving "animalcules" he had seen in semen; Leeuwenhoek recorded that Ham had seen them first.
- **c2** established: Leeuwenhoek reported the observations to the Royal Society in November 1677; the original letter is lost, but a translation was printed in the Philosophical Transactions.
