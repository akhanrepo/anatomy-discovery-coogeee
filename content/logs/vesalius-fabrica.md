First-pass verification from the October 2026 accuracy review (docs/research/accuracy-review-2026-10.md), then claim-by-claim against the sources listed.

- **c1** established: Vesalius published De humani corporis fabrica in Basel in 1543, illustrated with large woodcuts.
- **c2** established: Galen had described the lower jaw as two bones, based on animal anatomy; Vesalius showed that in humans it is a single bone.
- **c3** contested: Vesalius denied that the rete mirabile exists in humans, though the structure did not immediately disappear from anatomical writing after 1543.
