- **c1** established: In drawings of about 1512–13, Leonardo proposed that eddies forming in the sinuses behind the aortic valve cusps help close the valve after each heartbeat.
- **c2** established: The vortex mechanism was confirmed by modern studies, including MRI.
- **c3** contested: Whether Leonardo actually built the glass model of the aortic root he described is disputed by historians.
