First-pass verification from the October 2026 accuracy review (docs/research/accuracy-review-2026-10.md), then claim-by-claim against the sources listed.

- **c1** established: Using a modified version of Golgi's stain, Cajal showed in studies of the bird cerebellum and retina in 1888–89 that nerve cells are separate units rather than a continuous network.
- **c2** established: Golgi and Cajal shared the 1906 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine; in his Nobel lecture Golgi still defended the reticular theory.
- **c3** established: The neuron doctrine was given its name by Wilhelm Waldeyer in 1891, and electron microscopy later showed that a membrane encloses each neuron.
