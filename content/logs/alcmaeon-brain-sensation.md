First-pass verification from the October 2026 accuracy review (docs/research/accuracy-review-2026-10.md), then claim-by-claim against the sources listed.

- **c1** established: Alcmaeon identified the brain as the seat of understanding and distinguished understanding from perception.
- **c2** established: Theophrastus reports that Alcmaeon described channels (poroi) carrying sensations from the sense organs to the brain.
- **c3** contested: The claim that he dissected an eye comes from Calcidius, writing centuries later, and modern scholars doubt that dissection was a regular method for him.
