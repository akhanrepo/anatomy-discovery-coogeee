- **c1** established: In 2018 Benias, Theise and colleagues reported fluid-filled spaces within connective tissue throughout the body, which they argued had been missed because preparing tissue for slides drains the fluid and collapses them.
- **c2** contested: Press coverage called it a "new organ"; other anatomists welcomed the imaging but called the organ label a distraction, and later commentary argued the interstitium itself was not new.
