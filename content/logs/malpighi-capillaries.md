- **c1** established: In two letters to Giovanni Alfonso Borelli printed at Bologna in 1661, Malpighi showed with a microscope that the lung is made of tiny air sacs rather than solid flesh.
- **c2** established: Working on frogs with the help of Carlo Fracassati, he observed blood moving through minute vessels connecting arteries and veins in the lung, which Harvey had inferred but never seen.
