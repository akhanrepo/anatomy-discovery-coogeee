First-pass verification from the October 2026 accuracy review (docs/research/accuracy-review-2026-10.md), then claim-by-claim against the sources listed.

- **c1** established: Michael Servetus described the pulmonary circulation in Christianismi restitutio (1553), a theological book published anonymously and almost entirely destroyed, with only three copies surviving.
- **c2** established: Juan Valverde de Hamusco described the pulmonary circulation in print in 1556 in an anatomy book, crediting his teacher Realdo Colombo, whose own De re anatomica appeared in 1559.
- **c3** contested: Because Servetus's book was suppressed and Ibn al-Nafis's commentary was unprinted, there is no evidence either was available to Colombo, who appears to have reached his conclusion independently.
