First-pass verification from the October 2026 accuracy review (docs/research/accuracy-review-2026-10.md), then claim-by-claim against the sources listed.

- **c1** established: Falloppio's Observationes anatomicae (Venice, 1561) was a critical commentary on Vesalius's Fabrica that corrected and extended it, and the only work he published in his lifetime.
- **c2** established: He rejected Vesalius's account of the tube as a seed-carrying vessel and described it as a separate structure, naming it the uteri tuba for its trumpet shape.
