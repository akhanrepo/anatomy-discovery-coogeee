First-pass verification from the October 2026 accuracy review (docs/research/accuracy-review-2026-10.md), then claim-by-claim against the sources listed.

- **c1** established: Herophilus distinguished nerves from blood vessels and tendons, separated sensory from motor nerves, and described several pairs of cranial nerves.
- **c2** contested: The accusation that they vivisected condemned criminals rests on Celsus, writing about 250 years later, and on Tertullian; Galen, often critical of them, does not mention it, and many modern scholars doubt it.
