# Accuracy review: candidate discoveries (October 2026)

**Reviewer:** Claude (AI-assisted verification, see method in [F05](../features/F05-citations-and-contested-claims.md)) · **Date:** 2026-10-10 · **Scope:** the F04 longlist + the art sources named in F16

This is a **first-pass verification**. It checks that each headline claim is supported by at least one reputable source and hunts for the known myths. It's not the final per-claim citation work, which happens when each story is written (F05 workflow, steps 2–6). Where only popular or encyclopaedic sources were reachable, the verdict says so.

## Verdict key
- ✅ **Confirmed**: supported by scholarly or institutional sources; no significant dispute found.
- ⚠️ **Confirmed with framing**: core fact holds, but the date, attribution or "first" needs careful wording (copy rule given).
- 🔁 **Reframe**: the popular claim is wrong or a myth. Ship it as a *myth-correcting* story (status `revised`) or change what the story claims.
- ❌ **Dropped claim**: couldn't verify; removed from the brief until sourced.

## Myths confirmed as myths (to debunk, not repeat)
- **"Chinese medicine described blood circulation before Harvey."** This rests on a disputed translation (above).
- **"Sushruta lived in the 6th c. BCE."** The scholarly view is a layered text, begun no earlier than the last centuries BCE.
- **"Vesalius corrected 200 of Galen's errors."** A popular figure with no scholarly source found.
- **"Leeuwenhoek discovered sperm."** Johan Ham saw them first, and Leeuwenhoek said so.
- **"Leonardo proved the aortic valve mechanism with a glass model."** Whether he built it is disputed.
- **"The brain's lymphatic vessels were discovered in 2015."** They were described in 1787.
- **"The interstitium is a newly discovered organ."** The "organ" status is contested.

## Limits of this review
- Several key scholarly works (Meulenbeld, Unschuld, Nutton, Siraisi, Savage-Smith) are paywalled or print-only. Where a verdict relies on a *summary* of them, the story-writing pass must cite the work itself with a page locator, or downgrade the claim.
- "On this day" dates: only **22 May 1954** is verified so far. All others are open (F07).
