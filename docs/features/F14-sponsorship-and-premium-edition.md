# F14 — Sponsorship & premium book-style edition

**Priority:** Later · **Depends on:** audience; F04/F05 content depth; F06 rights

## Summary
Two monetisation options beyond classroom packs:
1. **Sponsorship**: a museum, library, medical college or education publisher sponsors the site or a thread ("The circulation thread, supported by ..."). Tasteful credit only, with no influence on content.
2. **Premium edition**: a long-form, book-style edition (web and/or print-on-demand, EPUB) with extended essays per thread, high-resolution plates and a full bibliography.

## Why not MVP
Both depend on proven audience and content depth. Sponsors want traffic and credibility, and a premium edition needs 3–5× the content of the MVP.

## Guardrails (decide now, enforce later)
- Editorial independence: no sponsor reviews or approves content. Publish this policy.
- Licences: the premium edition must be sellable, so keep no NC/SA assets in the core set (F06).

## Open questions
- Are pharma or medical-device sponsors acceptable, or out of bounds? (Credibility risk with the ICP.)
- Is there a book-publisher licensing route (the content itself as a print book) instead of self-publishing?
