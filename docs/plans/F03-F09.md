# Plan F03 + F09 — Story panel and misbeliefs

> Process note: written during implementation of the core batch.

**Approach.** Every story, misbelief and lens concept is server-rendered as a hidden `<article>`. The controller shows one, moves the year after the panel paints, switches layer and figure, highlights structures, and pushes history (so the back gesture closes the panel). On mobile the panel is a bottom-sheet dialog with a focus trap. Footnotes link to claim notes with sources, a verification date and a correction link. Misbeliefs are drawn in vermilion with `data-phase` (red, partial, erased) and can be toggled with "Show beliefs of the time". The token lint keeps vermilion to misbeliefs only.
