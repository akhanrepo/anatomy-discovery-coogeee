# Plan F01 + F13 — Body and layered dissection

> Process note: written during implementation of the core batch.

**Approach.** `src/art/figure.ts` holds male and female silhouettes plus 41 structures (area/line shapes) on 4 layers, keyed by content IDs. `src/art/render.ts` renders the SSR SVG, one stacked `.flap` per layer. Shallower flaps fold away with a CSS 3D `rotateY`, and deeper ones show faintly for orientation. Hit-testing: lines first and smaller areas on top, with narrow invisible hit strokes, and misbelief overlays are non-interactive. Keyboard: active-layer structures get `tabindex=0`, and Enter/Space opens the story. There's an accessible list view. Sex-specific structures are toggled by `data-fig`.
