# Plan F12 + F14 — Classroom packs, edition, sponsorship

**Approach.**
- **Packs:** `/packs/[id]/print/` (noindex) renders a printable pack from a thread. It has a cover (title, version, licence), 4 black-line plates at key years (our figure in print mode, to hand-colour), story cards, a "spot the myth" activity, discussion questions and an answer key with full sources. Every claim is shown with its claim ID. `scripts/build-artefacts.ts` prints A4 and Letter PDFs with Playwright into `artefacts/packs/` (not public). The free sample "Galen vs Vesalius" is published at `/packs/sample.pdf`.
- **Version:** `contentVersion()` is a short hash of all claims plus the date, shown on pack covers and the edition.
- **Commerce:** `/teachers/` and `/edition/` show prices from `site.commerce`. Buy buttons link to the Lemon Squeezy checkout URLs when configured; otherwise "Coming soon" with a notify link. `checkout_start` fires on click. There are no accounts. **Don't touch without asking** applies to the checkout config.
- **Edition:** `/edition/` shows the table of contents and a free preview chapter (circulation). The full edition is built by `scripts/build-artefacts.ts`: Markdown → pandoc EPUB + PDF (`artefacts/edition/`). The chapters are thread intros + stories + misbeliefs, with all claims cited.
- **Sponsorship:** `/support/` publishes the editorial-independence policy. The sponsor credit renders only if `site.sponsors` is non-empty **and** the policy page exists (build-time assertion).

**Risk:** PDF generation needs Chromium at build time, which Netlify's build image may not have. The artefacts script is separate from `npm run build`, so the site deploys without it; PDFs are produced locally or in CI and uploaded to the merchant.
